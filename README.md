# Invoice Simple — Day 03

Create a clean, printable invoice in your browser. Add business and client details, line items, tax, and a discount; the live preview updates as you type. Print it or choose **Save as PDF** in your browser’s print dialog.

**[Open Invoice Simple](https://mhussain-codes.github.io/day-03-invoice-maker/)** · [View source](https://github.com/mhussain-codes/day-03-invoice-maker)

## Features

- Add or remove service and product rows with quantity and rate calculations.
- Choose a currency, enter issue and due dates, and include payment instructions.
- Apply a discount before percentage tax; the discount is capped at the subtotal.
- Preview the complete invoice and use a print-friendly A4 layout.
- Reset the form to the sample invoice at any time.

## Use it

1. Open the live page in a modern browser.
2. Replace the sample details with your own and review the preview and total.
3. Select **Print / Save PDF**, then print or save the result as a PDF.

## Privacy and limitations

All fields and calculations stay in the current browser page. This project has no server, account, analytics, or browser storage. Reloading the page clears entered information. Check invoice details and tax treatment before sending: this is a formatting and arithmetic helper, not accounting or tax advice, and does not validate local invoice requirements.
